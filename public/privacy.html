Privacy Policy

Doodh Wala · Last updated 25 September 2026

Doodh Wala (“we”, “us”) is a milk delivery app for farmers and customers in India. This policy explains what we collect and why. Contact: gauravharal1001@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account: name, mobile number, password, and role (farmer, customer, or staff).
  • Farm and delivery data: farm profile, products, service areas, addresses, delivery records, bills, and payments you enter.
  • Location: approximate or precise location when you allow it, so nearby farms and delivery stops can be shown.
  • Photos: images you choose (camera or gallery), for example cash-payment proof.
  • Device: app version and basic crash/network logs needed to run the service. We do not sell this data.

How we use it

To create your account, connect farmers and customers, schedule and confirm deliveries, prepare bills, record payments, and send in-app notifications about those events.

Where it is stored

Data is stored on our cloud database and application servers (currently Neon and Render). It may be processed outside India. Access is limited to running the service. The app does not show ads today and we do not sell personal information. If we add ads later, we will update this policy first.

Sharing

Farmers see the customer details needed to deliver milk (name, address, phone, order). Customers see the farm details they subscribe to. We share data with hosting providers only to operate the app, or if the law requires it.

Your choices

  • You can edit profile and address information in the app.
  • You can turn off location or camera in Android settings. Some features will not work without them.
  • You can delete your account in Settings → Delete account, or email gauravharal1001@gmail.com. We then delete or anonymise account data that is not required for legal records (for example past bills).

Children

Doodh Wala is for people 18 years or older. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
