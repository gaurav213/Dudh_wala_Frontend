Delete your Doodh Wala account

Doodh Wala · Last updated 25 September 2026

You can delete your Doodh Wala account and the personal data linked to it. This page is for the Doodh Wala Android app and website.

How to request deletion

In the app (fastest):

  1. Open Doodh Wala and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. You will be signed out and cannot use that login again.

By email:

Write to gauravharal1001@gmail.com from the mobile number or email you use, and include that mobile number. We process email requests within 7 days.

What we delete

  • Your login (name, mobile number, password).
  • Profile, saved addresses, and farm profile you created.
  • App session tokens and local data on the device after you delete from Settings.

What we may keep, and for how long

Delivery, bill, and payment records that are required for tax or dispute history may be kept in anonymised or farm-ledger form for up to 7 years. Those records will no longer show your name or mobile number as an active account.
